## How to Use

1. **Go to Admin Panel**: Navigate to `admin.html` or click the "Admin" link in the navbar
2. **Login**: Use `admin` / `admin123`
3. **Manage Products**:
   - View all products in Products tab
   - Search for specific products
   - Click "Edit" to modify product details (description, price, image)
   - Click "Delete" to remove products
   - Go to "Add Product" tab to create new products

## Data Storage
